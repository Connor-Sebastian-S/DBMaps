The 2000–01 scheme, designed by Macmon Architects, refurbished the historic core and added a substantial contemporary extension. It was completed in April/June 2001 (sources differ slightly on the formal completion date). A fully glazed, double-height link connects the old building to the new facilities. The extension has a steel frame and a radiused plan wrapped in vertical western red cedar boards, intended to weather naturally to silver-grey. New facilities included a foyer/cafe, multi-purpose hall/auditorium, arts workshops and mezzanine gallery. The organisation changed its name to Birnam Arts in September 2011; that was a name change, not another major exterior building phase.
